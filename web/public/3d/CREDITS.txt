Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database
License (ODbL) 1.0. https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
Changes: buildings extruded from their tagged or estimated heights; lit
windows, facades, generic rooftop signs (no real brand), street lights and
traffic are invented for the render.

cabin-architecture.jpg (also embedded in cabin.glb)
Generated with the built-in imagegen tool, 2026-10-16. Fictional architectural
photograph for the framed print in the cabin; not a photograph of this building.
Converted to a 768 px JPEG for the project. Original generated PNG preserved.
Prompt:
Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: a photographic print texture to be
placed inside a small real 3D frame on an office elevator wall. Create a single
understated black-and-white architectural photograph of an ordinary mid-century
European office building, viewed from pavement level looking slightly upwards,
showing a concrete facade, repetitive window bays and a mature tree partly
crossing one corner. Editorial analog photography, authentic weathered concrete,
subtle photographic grain, soft overcast daylight, quiet everyday atmosphere.
Portrait 2:3 composition. Only the photograph itself, full bleed, no frame, no
mat, no elevator, no poster mockup, no text, no logos, no watermarks. Restrained
tonal range with natural gray midtones, no dramatic cinematic lighting, no
artificial CGI polish.

Cabin floor (embedded in cabin.glb)
Source: ambientCG, Marble021, CC0.

Changes: 2K color, OpenGL normal and roughness maps embedded as WebP;
pinwheel layout of five slabs on the floor and five panels on the ceiling,
each with its own offset in the 3 m texture. Native white color, subtle
normal strength, satin roughness and slight entrance traffic polish.
